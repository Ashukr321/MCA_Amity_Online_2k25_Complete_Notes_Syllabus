# 4. Objective(s) of the Study

The purpose of undertaking this project is to examine, through secondary research, how Artificial
Intelligence is changing recruitment technology. Specific objectives are:

1. **To study the industry trend** of AI adoption in recruitment platforms and the business problem
   it addresses (screening volume, inconsistency, and bias in manual resume review).
2. **To examine the typical architecture** of an AI-powered recruitment platform — frontend,
   backend, database, and AI processing pipeline — as representative of current HR-tech practice.
3. **To analyze weighted, multi-factor scoring** as a matching methodology, and evaluate it against
   secondary sources on resume-parsing and candidate-ranking approaches.
4. **To assess non-functional considerations** relevant to such systems: security and access control,
   scalability, and AI cost governance.
5. **To identify practical recommendations** for organizations or student teams building or adopting
   similar AI-driven recruitment systems, based on the wider literature.
